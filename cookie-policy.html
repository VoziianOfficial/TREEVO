Cookie overview

TREEVO may use cookies and similar technologies to run the website, remember basic choices, improve navigation, understand general website activity, and support contact request functionality.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. Similar technologies can include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and analytics identifiers.

These tools can help a website remember choices, measure usage, improve performance, and support basic functionality.

2. Types of cookies we may use

TREEVO may use different categories of cookies depending on website functionality, your cookie choices, and available tools.

Cookie type Purpose Required?
Essential cookies Help the website function, remember cookie banner choices, support security, and keep basic page features working. Usually required
Preference cookies Remember choices such as cookie decisions, interface preferences, or repeated website settings. Optional where applicable
Analytics cookies Help understand general website usage, page performance, navigation patterns, and content improvement opportunities. Optional where applicable
Functional cookies Support enhanced website interactions such as form behavior, UI state, or user experience improvements. Optional where applicable

3. How TREEVO may use cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support the TREEVO website and provider comparison experience.

  • To remember cookie banner accept or decline choices.
  • To help pages load and function correctly across devices.
  • To improve navigation, layout, forms, and overall website usability.
  • To understand general traffic, page visits, and interaction behavior.
  • To help identify technical errors, performance issues, or broken flows.
  • To support basic security, misuse prevention, and website integrity.
Provider comparison note: TREEVO is an independent comparison platform. Cookies do not mean TREEVO performs tree care work directly or controls independent provider services.

4. Third-party tools

TREEVO may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, performance monitoring, communication support, or website tools. These third parties may place or read cookies and similar technologies according to their own policies.

Third-party cookies are controlled by the relevant third party, not by TREEVO. You should review the privacy and cookie practices of those services where applicable.

5. Cookie duration

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose.

  • Session cookies may expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies may remain for a set period or until deleted.
  • Preference cookies may remain longer so your choices can be remembered.

6. Your cookie controls

You may control cookies through the website cookie banner where available and through your browser settings.

  • You can accept cookies through the cookie banner.
  • You can decline non-essential cookies where the banner provides that option.
  • You can delete cookies from your browser settings.
  • You can block cookies using browser controls, though some website features may not work correctly.
  • You can use private browsing or browser-level tracking protection tools.
Important: Blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality, form behavior, cookie banner storage, and basic navigation features.

7. Browser settings

Most browsers let you view, manage, delete, or block cookies. The exact steps depend on the browser and device you use.

Browser controls may allow you to delete existing cookies, block future cookies, receive cookie warnings, or limit third-party cookies. Changes may affect this and other websites.

8. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers offer “Do Not Track” or similar signals. Because there is no single industry standard for responding to these signals, TREEVO may not respond to every such browser setting in the same way.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the updated Cookie Policy.
