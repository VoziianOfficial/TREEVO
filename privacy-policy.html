Note

TREEVO is a provider comparison platform. We do not perform tree removal, trimming, stump grinding, land clearing, or inspection work directly.

Privacy overview

TREEVO helps homeowners compare local tree care provider options. This policy describes the types of information we may collect when you visit the website, use contact forms, request provider options, interact with cookie tools, or communicate with us.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the website.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, information generated through your use of the website, and limited technical information used to operate and improve the site.

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and similar contact details.
  • Tree care service category, urgency, property details, and project description.
  • Messages submitted through forms, email links, or phone-related contact actions.
  • Consent confirmations related to contact requests, terms, and provider comparison.

Website usage information

  • Pages viewed, buttons clicked, links visited, and general interaction behavior.
  • Approximate device, browser, operating system, and referral information.
  • Cookie preferences, cookie banner choices, and basic session details.
Important: Please avoid submitting sensitive information that is not needed for a tree care provider comparison request.

2. How we may use information

We may use collected information to operate the TREEVO website, respond to requests, organize provider comparison inquiries, improve user experience, and maintain basic website security.

  • To respond to tree care provider comparison requests.
  • To understand the service category, urgency, location, and project details you submit.
  • To communicate with you about your request, where permitted.
  • To improve website layout, navigation, performance, and content clarity.
  • To prevent misuse, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain site security.
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests where applicable.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

TREEVO may use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies to remember cookie choices, support website functionality, understand general site usage, and improve user experience.

Type Purpose
Essential cookies Support basic website functionality, cookie preference storage, and security.
Analytics cookies Help understand how visitors use the site so pages can be improved.
Preference cookies Remember choices such as cookie banner decisions or interface preferences.

For more detail, review our Cookie Policy.

4. How information may be shared

TREEVO may share information only as needed to operate the website, respond to your request, support provider comparison workflows, use service providers, or comply with legal obligations.

  • With service providers that help operate the website, hosting, analytics, or communication tools.
  • With independent provider-related parties where necessary to help process a comparison request.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and website integrity.
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, restructuring, or similar transaction.
Provider independence: Providers referenced through TREEVO are independent. TREEVO does not control their privacy practices. Review any provider’s own policies before sharing additional details.

5. Data retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, operate the website, keep business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and improve website functionality.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose of collection, legal requirements, and operational needs.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to help protect information. However, no website, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for submitting accurate information and avoiding unnecessary sensitive details in website forms.

7. Your choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have choices or rights related to your information. These may include requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or opt-out options where available.

  • You can choose not to submit a contact form.
  • You can use cookie banner options where available.
  • You can contact us to request information about your submitted details.
  • You can avoid sharing project information that is not necessary for provider comparison.

8. Children’s privacy

The TREEVO website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the website, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
